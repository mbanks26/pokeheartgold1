# iPhone test checklist

Apply the latest BPS patch to your own clean US HeartGold ROM with Safari's
[Rom Patcher JS](https://www.marcrobledo.com/RomPatcher.js/). Source SHA-1:
4fcded0e2713dc03929845de631d0932ea2b5a37. Keep your existing ROM and battery
save backed up; import the patched ROM as a separate Manic EMU test entry.
Load native battery saves only. Emulator save states belong to their exact ROM.

- Start a new male game, choose your own name, approach Elm's lab, watch the
  family scene and sister's walking departure. Complete all native tutorials.
- Choose any starter. Check its real shiny summary and first battle animation.
- Check protagonist, Sabrina and sister appearance, walking, special actions,
  Trainer Card and intro artwork. Existing graphics were preserved.
- Walk, turn, wait, use doors, ledges, narrow passages, stairs and cave entrances.
  Sabrina follows behind the native Pokemon after a few safe walking tiles.
- Cycle/surf, return to walking, enter a scripted scene, battle and save/load.
  Sabrina must return without duplicates or blocked movement.
- Stand still on foot and press Select with Sabrina. Confirm dialogue changes naturally
  after story milestones and A still talks to the Pokemon/NPC in front.
- Meet the sister in Violet, Goldenrod, Ecruteak and Blackthorn. Decline and
  accept optional battles. Check her growing team and explained Eevee; Bill's
  original player gift must still work independently.
- At Slowpoke Well and Radio Tower, check the partner battle presentation. Give
  only your own Pokemon a command. Sabrina must act independently with her own
  party. Check victory, loss/retry and native story rewards/progression.
- Complete Saffron Gym normally: solo player artwork, official Sabrina team,
  Marsh Badge/TM reward, companion restoration outside. Lose once and retry.
- After Marsh Badge, talk to the two students near Saffron Gym for optional
  partner practice. Check native Singles elsewhere and Lance's original battle.
- Report visible clipping, wrong text timing, unexpected menus, missing actors,
  repeated rewards or crashes with the map/action and a Manic screenshot.

Headless DeSmuME checks do not establish full-campaign or Manic compatibility.
Link trading needs separate hardware/network support and remains a manual check.

The automated regression report is [STAGE-F.md](STAGE-F.md). The partner rear-view
presentation and a full campaign still need visual gameplay review on your device.
