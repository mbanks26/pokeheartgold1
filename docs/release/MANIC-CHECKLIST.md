# Manic EMU player-test checklist

These are ordinary gameplay checks, not requests to edit source or use a terminal.
Cloud native/static results are in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md); checking a box here
requires actual iPhone gameplay. Keep a backup before testing an older battery save.

## First session

- [ ] Import RC1 under a new name; start male, enter any desired player name.
- [ ] Check dreadlocks, skin, jacket, introduction artwork and Trainer Card.
- [ ] Meet the sister and Sabrina outside Elm's laboratory; verify natural dialogue.
- [ ] Choose a starter; check its shiny appearance/animation and ordinary moves.
- [ ] Walk far enough for both followers: player → Pokémon → Sabrina.
- [ ] Walk/run/turn/stop repeatedly, around corners, past NPCs and along fences.
- [ ] Enter/exit houses, Elm's lab and a Pokémon Center; cross Route 29; jump ledges.
- [ ] Finish Mr. Pokémon, rival, Egg delivery, Pokédex and catching tutorial.
- [ ] Save using the game's menu, completely close the game, reopen and Continue.
- [ ] Confirm name, starter/shiny status, story progress and exactly one Sabrina.
- [ ] Check the Bag's known stock 3D model; report any additional unexpected stock art.

## Movement and battles

- [ ] Cycle/dismount, Surf/land and cast the fishing rod; Sabrina hides and returns
      after several valid walking steps. Pokémon behavior remains native.
- [ ] Check stairs, caves, bridges, narrow passages, doors, warps, Fly and HM actions.
- [ ] Let NPCs approach during required scenes; neither follower should block progress.
- [ ] Fight wild and ordinary trainers; inspect combined player/Sabrina throw poses.
- [ ] Throw a Poké Ball; check animation and a successful catch.
- [ ] Complete all three Sabrina partner events; command only your own Pokémon.
- [ ] In partner battles test both sides fainting, legal targeting, switching,
      status/abilities, EXP/rewards, victory, defeat and formation afterward.
- [ ] Press Select while standing on foot with Sabrina present; check context,
      pagination and repeated visits without unsolicited entry popups/spoilers.

## Story and persistence

- [ ] Play all Johto milestones listed in the coverage table, including all eight
      badges, Rocket events, Dragon's Den, League and Champion.
- [ ] Visit sister encounters in Violet, Goldenrod, Ecruteak and Blackthorn.
      Review her independent goals, teasing, optional battles and chronology.
- [ ] Acquire her Eevee scene, revisit it, save/reload and confirm no duplicate reward.
      Obtain the player's separate Bill Eevee as well; sister Eevee stays unevolved.
- [ ] Revisit towns after battles and after a real day/week/time-of-day change.
      Companion/story status and badges must persist while daily events reset.
- [ ] Complete Kanto, Power Plant/Cerulean, all badges, Mount Silver and Red.
- [ ] Challenge official Sabrina: she is the enemy, not also beside the player;
      Marsh Badge/TM rewards and rejoining follow victory. Test loss/retry too.
- [ ] Obtain her phone appointment naturally and test the scheduled Dojo rematch.
- [ ] Save before/after Gym and partner battles, then fully close/reopen the game.
- [ ] In a separate female new game, check custom art/naming and native early
      progression, no added sister/Sabrina duplicates, and neutral Goldenrod breeder.
- [ ] Review cycling, Surf, fishing, Rocket disguise, Pokéathlon and other special
      graphics for palette, alignment, frame order and transparency.

When reporting an issue, include RC1, location, preceding action, expected versus
actual behavior, whether Continue or a save state was used, and a screenshot or
screen recording if useful. Keep a separate save before a reproducible problem.
Do not interpret an untested box as a known failure.
